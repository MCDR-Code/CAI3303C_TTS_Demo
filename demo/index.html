A small demo, with a clear scope

Sky matches questions against six predefined FAQ topics: its identity, languages, speech recognition, voice generation, locations, and student creators. This is not a general college help desk. English and Spanish answers are supported; English is preferred for FAQ matching and F5-TTS voice output. Spoken Spanish can be translated into English for matching. If Sky cannot find an answer, this demo cannot connect you to a real person.
